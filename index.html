The method

  1. 9:15 to 10:15 IST: read the market. NIFTY 50 against yesterday's close and its own open, how many stocks are up and down, and India VIX. NIFTY up 0.25% or more: bullish, buys only. Down 0.25% or more: bearish, short sells only. In between: neutral, no trade (in 12 months of testing, neutral days lost money).
  2. 10:15: shortlist stocks up (buy) or down (short) 1% to 2% from yesterday's close (the default, tested). Setting Move measured → Within the first hour uses your rule instead: stocks that moved 1.5% to 2% between 9:15 and 10:15, with NIFTY judged on its own first hour. In testing that rule lost money (12 months: 61 trades, 45.9% wins, −₹1,387; last 40 days: −₹2,095), so it is an option, not the default.
  3. Score each chart out of 100: VWAP side (25), close near the first-hour high or low (20), EMA 9 vs EMA 20 (15), volume against its usual first hour (20), stronger or weaker than NIFTY (10), 20-day trend (10). Standard mode needs 70+, high-accuracy 80+.
  4. Entry: the first 5-minute candle that closes beyond the first-hour high (buy) or low (short), by 2:30 PM.
  5. Stop: the first-hour midpoint, on the NSE tick. Target: by your risk : reward (1 : 2 by default, 2× the risk). Once the trade is 1R up, the stop moves to the entry (no loss after that).
  6. Exit: target, stop, or 3:10 PM, whichever comes first. Every trade closes the same day (intraday, MIS).

Your profit goal, the stop-loss and the target

You set the desired profit per trade and the risk : reward ratio (1 : 2 by default). Money fetches the stop-loss from the chart (the first-hour midpoint), sets the target at 2× the risk (for 1 : 2) away from the entry, and works out the quantity that makes your desired profit at the target. The most you can lose at the stop is the desired profit ÷ 2 (₹1,000 at 1 : 2 risks ₹500). The Trade calculator tab does the same for any NSE stock you type.

Alerts

Hour trend (Strategy → Hour trend)

A second trade after the first hour. At 12:15 PM Money reads the 11:15 AM to 12:15 PM hour: NIFTY must be at least ±0.25% on yesterday and have moved the same way during that hour (up: buys only, down: short sells only, otherwise no trade). It shortlists stocks 1% to 3% on yesterday whose 11:15–12:15 hour also moved that way, needs a chart score of 80+, and picks the best. Entry on a 5-minute close beyond that hour's high (buy) or low (short), stop at the hour's midpoint, target 1:2, stop to entry at +1R, out by 3:10 PM. Tested: 12 months of 1-hour bars, 63 trades, 54% wins, profit factor 1.97, +₹3,671 (profitable in both the tuning months and the 4 unseen months); last 40 days on 5-minute bars, 20 trades, 65% wins, +₹2,486. The 10:15–11:15 and 12:15–1:15 hours did not hold up, so Money uses only 11:15–12:15. It can run on the same day as the first-hour trade (both together over 12 months: +₹10,226).

Click 🔔 Alerts (top right) and allow notifications. While the Money page stays open on a trading day it watches by itself: a notice at 9:10, the pick (or "no trade") after 10:15, ENTRY with the quantity, stop-loss and target to place, +1R (move the stop to the entry), TARGET HIT or STOP HIT, "no entry" at 2:30 PM and CLOSE NOW at 3:05 PM. Each alert comes once, with a sound, a banner and a Mac notification. Prices are free Yahoo data, a few minutes delayed: your Zerodha orders are what actually protect you.

Size and charges

Shares = the smallest of: (desired profit ÷ reward multiple) ÷ risk per share, 75% of the budget, and the budget less a 10% cash buffer. Charges are Zerodha's equity intraday charges: brokerage ₹20 or 0.03% per order, STT 0.025% on the sell, exchange and SEBI fees, stamp duty and GST.

Honest limits

Free Yahoo Finance data: delayed by a few minutes and unofficial. 5-minute data covers the last 60 days; the 12-month test uses 1-hour bars, which delay the entry by up to an hour. Results on the past are not a promise. Most people who day trade lose money. Money never places orders: paper-trade first. Not financial advice.
